## 💡 Key Features

### Dashboard
- Real-time risk statistics
- Interactive pie chart (risk distribution)
- Stacked bar chart (risks by category)
- 5×5 heat map (Likelihood × Impact)
- Recent risks table

### Asset Management
- Add/Edit/Delete assets
- Search and filter
- Asset classification (System/Data/Hardware)
- Value assessment (Low/Medium/High/Critical)

### Threat Management
- Link threats to assets
- Document vulnerabilities
- Assess likelihood and impact
- Auto-calculate risk levels

### Risk Assessment Calculator
- **Qualitative**: Risk = Likelihood × Impact
- **Quantitative**: ALE = SLE × ARO (financial)
- **Hybrid**: Combined with control effectiveness

### Reports
- Complete risk register
- Top 5 highest risks
- Executive summary
- Print/PDF export (mocked)
